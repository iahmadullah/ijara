How it works

How ijara works

A co op of investors buys the property on your behalf and leases it to you until ownership transfers, riba free.

We buy it together.

You pick the home. A trust buys it outright using your contribution plus investor capital. No money is loaned-a property is bought.

You rent & buy in

Each month you pay rent on the share you don't own yet, plus an equity payment. As your share grows, the rent shrinks.

The title become yours

Once you have bought out the remaining share, the deed transfers. No interest ever charged, no penalty for finishing early.

Frequently asked questions

Have more questions? Don't hesitate to reach us

Our team answers by phone or email during business hours, and a pre-qualification takes about ten minutes.

How is this different from a mortgage?

ijara CDC co-owns the property with you and sells you its share over time. There is no interest, because there is no debt instrument — the agreement transfers ownership, not money at a rate.

What does a monthly payment cover?

Two parts: your use of the share ijara CDC still owns, and the purchase of an additional slice of that share. The second part grows as your ownership grows.

How long does pre-qualification take?

About ten minutes, and it does not affect your credit.

Can I pay off my share early?

Yes. Additional payments buy more of the co-owned share and carry no penalty.

Which states does ijara CDC serve?

More than 20 states across the USA, alongside Canada. Contact us for the current list and the requirements where you live.

Who reviews the contracts?

The Sharia Advisory Board and real estate counsel review the structure and the documents before signing.

What can I finance?

Residential and commercial property, residential and auto conversions, investment opportunities, and the ijaraCDC partner broker program.
